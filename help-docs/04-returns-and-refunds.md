# Returns and Refunds

Last updated: October 2026

All return and refund requests are reviewed and approved by our Customer Care team. Our support assistant can't approve refunds, but it will pass your request to the team. To speed things up, include your order number, the product, and the reason for the return.

## 30-day return window

You can request a return within 30 days of the delivery date.

## Unopened products

Unopened and unused products can be returned for a full refund of the product price. Original shipping costs aren't refunded.

- US: we email you a prepaid return label. $6.95 is deducted from your refund for the label.
- Canada: please ship the return yourself with a tracked service. Return shipping costs aren't refunded.

## Opened products: our Skin Happiness Promise

If a product isn't right for your skin, you can return it opened within 30 days of delivery. The bottle must be at least half full. This applies once per product per customer. The same return shipping rules apply.

## Damaged, leaking or wrong items

Tell us within 7 days of delivery and send a photo of the item and the packing slip. We'll send a free replacement or give a full refund, including shipping. You don't need to send the item back.

## Skin reactions

If a product irritates your skin, stop using it and rinse with cool water. If you have swelling, blistering or trouble breathing, get medical care right away.

Then contact us. Every skin reaction report is handled personally by our Customer Care team on the same business day, and the product is refunded in full no matter how much has been used. You don't need to send it back.

## Items that can't be returned

- Gift cards
- Items marked "Final Sale"

## Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. Please return the item and place a new order.

## When you'll get your refund

Refunds go to the original payment method within 3 business days after your return arrives at our warehouse, or after the team approves a refund that doesn't need a return. Your bank may take another 5 to 10 business days to show it.

## Refill Club orders

Refill Club orders follow this same policy. If you were charged for a Refill Club order you didn't want, contact us. If it hasn't shipped yet, the team will cancel it and refund it in full. If it has already shipped, the normal return policy applies.
