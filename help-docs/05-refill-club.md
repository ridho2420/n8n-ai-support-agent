# Refill Club (Subscriptions)

Last updated: October 2026

## What it is

The Refill Club sends your favorite products automatically on a schedule you choose.

- 15% off every Refill Club order
- Free shipping in the US on every Refill Club order. Canada: $6.95 per order.
- Choose a delivery schedule of every 30, 60 or 90 days for each product
- No minimum number of orders and no fees. Cancel anytime.

All full-size products can be added to the Refill Club. The Starter Trio and gift cards can't.

## Payment

Your card is charged on the morning of the day each Refill Club order ships, not before. We email you a reminder 5 days before each order ships.

## Managing your subscription

Log in to your account on our website and open the Refill Club page. There you can:

- skip the next order
- change the next ship date
- change how often each product arrives
- swap or add products
- pause for up to 3 months
- cancel

Changes must be made at least 3 days before the next ship date. Within 3 days of shipping, the order is already being prepared and can't be changed online. Contact us if you need help.

## Discounts

The 15% Refill Club discount can't be combined with other discount codes.

## Forgot your password?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. If the reset email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or contact us.
