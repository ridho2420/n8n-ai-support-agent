# Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: October 2026

## Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes. We never test our products or ingredients on animals, and neither do our suppliers.

## Are your products vegan?

All products are vegan except Barrier Balm Moisturizer, which contains beeswax.

## Are your products fragrance-free?

All products are fragrance-free except Weekend Glow Exfoliating Toner, which has a light grapefruit scent from grapefruit peel oil.

## Where are your products made?

All products are formulated and made in the USA.

## Do you have a physical store?

No. We sell only on our website. We don't have a store or showroom, and other retailers don't sell our products.

## Which products are right for my skin type?

- Oily or combination skin: Tidepool Gel Cleanser, Dew Drop Hydrating Serum, Weekend Glow Exfoliating Toner
- Dry or sensitive skin: Oat Cloud Cream Cleanser, Dew Drop Hydrating Serum, Barrier Balm Moisturizer
- Every skin type: Daily Shield Mineral Sunscreen SPF 40

New to Saltgrove? The Starter Trio is an easy way to try our basics.

## Should I patch test?

Yes, we recommend it for every new product. Apply a small amount to your inner arm or behind your ear once a day for 3 days. If you see redness or itching, don't use the product on your face.

## Can I use Night Shift Retinal Serum and Weekend Glow Exfoliating Toner together?

Not on the same night. Use them on different evenings.

## Can I use your products while pregnant or breastfeeding, or with a skin condition or prescription medication?

We can't give medical advice. Please check with your doctor or dermatologist. These questions are always answered by a person on our Customer Care team, who can also send the full ingredient list for any product.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Shop Pay.

Unopened: 24 months. After opening: 12 months, except Night Shift Retinal Serum (6 months). See the Product Catalog.
