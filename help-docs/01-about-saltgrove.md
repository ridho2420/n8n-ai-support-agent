# About Saltgrove Skin

Last updated: October 2026

## Who we are

Saltgrove Skin is a small skincare brand founded in 2021 in Portland, Oregon. We make a short routine of gentle, effective products for everyday skin: two cleansers, a hydrating serum, a retinal serum, a moisturizer, a mineral sunscreen and an exfoliating toner. All products are formulated and made in the USA.

We sell only through our own website. We don't have a physical store or showroom, and other retailers don't sell our products.

## Where we ship

We ship to the United States and Canada. See the Shipping Policy for rates and delivery times.

## How to reach us

- Email: write to our support email address, or reply to any email you received from us.
- Chat: use the chat window on our website.

Both go to the same support team.

## How our support works

Our customer support assistant replies to messages at any time of day. It can:

- answer questions about our products, shipping, returns and the Refill Club, using our help documents
- check the status of an order when you give the order number and the email address used to place it

Some things are always handled by a person on our Customer Care team:

- refund and return requests
- damaged, leaking or wrong items
- skin reactions (treated as urgent)
- questions about pregnancy, breastfeeding, medical conditions or prescription medication
- changes to an order or a shipping address
- anything the assistant can't answer from our help documents

When a message is passed to the team, you'll get a reply confirming that a team member will follow up.

## Customer Care hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Pacific Time. Closed on US public holidays.

A team member replies within 1 business day. Skin reaction reports are answered on the same business day.
