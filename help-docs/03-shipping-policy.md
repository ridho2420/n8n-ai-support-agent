# Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. We don't ship to other countries yet.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 12:00 pm Pacific Time on a business day (Monday to Friday) usually ship the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and US public holidays, ship on the next business day.

## Shipping options and rates

United States:

- Standard (USPS Ground Advantage): 3 to 6 business days after shipping. $5.95, or free on orders of $50 or more.
- Express (UPS 2nd Day Air): 2 business days after shipping. $14.95. Not available for PO boxes.

Canada:

- Standard (tracked): 6 to 12 business days after shipping. $12.95, or free on orders of $75 or more.
- Import duties and taxes may be collected by the carrier on delivery. These are paid by the customer.

Order totals for free shipping are counted after discounts and before tax. Refill Club orders have their own shipping rates (see the Refill Club document).

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also ask our support assistant for your order status. Give your order number (for example SG-10234) and the email address you used to place the order.

## Changing your order or address

We can usually change or cancel an order, including the shipping address, if you contact us within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order may already be packed and can't be changed. Order changes are handled by our Customer Care team.

## Late, lost or missing packages

- If your tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days (US) or 10 business days (Canada), contact us. We'll open a trace with the carrier and send a free replacement if the package is lost.
- If tracking shows "delivered" but you can't find the package, please wait 48 hours and check with neighbors or your building's mailroom. Then contact us and our Customer Care team will help.

## Wrong address entered

If a package comes back to us because of an address error, we'll contact you to reship it. Reshipping is charged at the normal shipping rate.
